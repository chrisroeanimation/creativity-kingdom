PICTURES ON THE BOARDS IN THE PARK
==================================

Put a picture in this folder with one of the names below and it replaces the picture on that
board in the park. Delete the file to go back to the built-in picture. .jpg, .png and .webp work.

  File name          Board                                   Shape (width : height)
  showreel.jpg       next to the Showreel Coaster            16:9 landscape
  clayjam.jpg        Clay Jam                                square
  chaos.jpg          Chaos Castle                            square
  makes.jpg          Marvellous Makes                        square
  makeslab.jpg       Marvellous Makes Lab (inside the ride)  16:9 landscape
  creaturebox.jpg    Creature Box                            square
  strike.jpg         Strike                                  portrait, about 7:10 (a film poster)
  sky.jpg            Sky Kids Christmas                      square
  weasel.jpg         Wattle Weasels                          16:9 landscape
  portrait.jpg       your photo at the hub                   square

The picture is stretched to fill the board, so crop it to the shape listed first.
About 1200 px on the long side is plenty.
