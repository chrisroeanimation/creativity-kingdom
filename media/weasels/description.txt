The stupefying courtship display of a seductive wattle weasel.

Inspired by nature documentaries and their footage of unusual mating rituals, I invented a species with a wonderfully absurd courtship display.

The film combines live-action puppetry with stop motion, using a single puppet shot many times with interchangeable wattles, beaks and legs. I motion-tracked the puppet footage and added expressive CG eyes, the same practical-plus-digital approach I used on a freelance rail-safety film for Siemens.
