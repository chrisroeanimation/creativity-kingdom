I created the concept, the characters and the handmade world of Clay Jam, a ridiculously happy mobile action game where every creature, prop and hillside is 100% plasticine.

I sculpted the peculiar world and its whimsical clay inhabitants by hand, animated them in stop motion, and turned the animation into photographic sprites for the game engine.

As a founder of Fat Pebble, a 24-person studio, I led its art and animation department of eight and set the visual rules everyone built to. The game was nominated in the Family category at the 2013 BAFTA Games Awards and has reached more than 10 million players.

Now at YumYumYukYuk, I'm creating the world for Clay Jam 2, currently in production.
