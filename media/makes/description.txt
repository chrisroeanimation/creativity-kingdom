Marvellous Makes brings to life the love and joy of creating things with plasticine and simple household objects. It isn't about making things perfectly; it's about getting creative and having a go.

The series combines pixilation, where live hands are animated frame by frame like a stop-motion puppet, with stop-motion characters, so everything, even the make itself, comes to life. Two series for children aged 3 to 9 were shown on Sky Kids, Azoomee and Da Vinci Kids.

Its spin-off, Marvellous Makes Lab, combines science and creativity with experiments you can do at home, from an air cannon to a lava lamp to a paper helicopter that really flies.

I created and produced Marvellous Makes, directing alongside Lauren Sandercock and Matt Wierenga, and built the physical sets, sourced the props and directed the lighting, leading a team of five artists and animators.
